## 🆘 Troubleshooting

**No flows in Kafka?**
- Check CICFlowMeter logs
- Verify network traffic exists
- Test tcpdump manually

**DDoS Detector not alerting?**
- Check threshold settings in `detection_rules.yaml`
- Lower `min_flows` for testing
- Generate test attack traffic

**High false positives?**
- Increase `min_suspicious_ratio`
- Add IPs to whitelist
- Adjust confidence thresholds
